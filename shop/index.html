For store owners

Give every product a fitting room.

This store is a working demo of what I can build into yours. Shoppers who see a piece on themselves don’t have to guess how it looks, so they buy with more confidence.

  • Clothes, shoes, hats, glasses

    Tops and jackets, dresses, denim, footwear, headwear, eyewear and accessories, each with its own framing.

  • Live video, not a still photo

    The shopper turns and moves, and the item follows at 720p. Swap pieces mid-session in one tap.

  • Runs in the browser

    Phone or laptop, no app, no download. It sits on your existing product page and cards.

  • $Pay per second of use

    The model bills $0.02 per live second, so a 30-second try-on costs about $0.60. Your key stays on your server.

Your catalog pageVue

<ProductCard
  v-for="p in products"
  :product="p"
  @try-on="tryOn(p)"
/>

<!-- one fitting room for the whole store -->
<TryOn :product="trying" :products="products" />

What each product needs3 fields

{
  image: '/products/moto-jacket.jpg',
  kind: 'top',          // what it swaps: top, bottom, outfit,
                        // shoes, hat, glasses, accessory
  note: 'a black leather biker jacket'
}

That’s how this page is built: every card hands its photo and garment type to one shared fitting room. On Shopify, WooCommerce or your own stack, it becomes a button on the product page.
