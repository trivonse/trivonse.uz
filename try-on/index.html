i Getting a good result

The model works best from one clean garment, ideally on a plain background. Most pins show a whole outfit on a person, so draw a box around the one piece you want, then say what it is.

ii What it costs

Decart bills your key about $0.02 for every second the try-on runs. It stops after five minutes, or once the page has been out of sight for a minute.

iii Privacy

Your camera and the garment go straight from this browser to Decart while a session runs. Nothing passes through this site. Your garments and photos stay in this browser until you delete them. The site counts visits and clicks with PostHog, but never your camera, your images, your key or what you type. Hand signs are read on your device; the camera picture isn’t sent anywhere for them. Only try on with your own face, or with someone who has said yes.
